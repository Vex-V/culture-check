Does your video speak to the audience you made it for?

Culture Check finds the brands, places, clothes and settings in your video and shows which age groups each one reaches, using Qloo's audience data.

  1. 01
    Read the video. It describes each shot and transcribes the sound to find the cues.
  2. 02
    Ask Qloo who each cue reaches. Each cue gets its audience by age and gender, and its following in your city.
  3. 03
    Fix what misses. It suggests replacements, writes a brief and answers follow-up questions.
